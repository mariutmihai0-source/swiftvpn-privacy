Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2, 2026

SwiftVPN respects your privacy and is committed to protecting personal information. This Privacy Policy explains what information may be processed when you use the SwiftVPN mobile application.

1. VPN Service

SwiftVPN provides a virtual private network (VPN) service using an encrypted VPN tunnel. When you connect to SwiftVPN, your internet traffic is routed through a SwiftVPN VPN server. The VPN server necessarily processes your IP address and connection information in order to establish and maintain the encrypted VPN connection.

SwiftVPN does not intentionally inspect, record, or store the content of your internet traffic or your browsing history.

2. Information We May Process

To operate the VPN service, SwiftVPN may temporarily process technical information such as:

  • Your public IP address
  • VPN connection status
  • Connection timestamps
  • VPN server information
  • Basic technical and diagnostic information required to maintain the service

This information is used only for operating, securing, troubleshooting, and improving the VPN service.

3. Advertising

The free version of SwiftVPN may display advertisements provided by Google AdMob. Google and its advertising partners may process information such as device identifiers, IP address, advertising identifiers, approximate location derived from IP address, ad impressions and interactions, device and application information, diagnostic information, and user consent choices.

Users located in the European Economic Area (EEA), United Kingdom, and Switzerland may be shown a consent message before advertising data is processed where required by applicable law.

4. VPN Traffic and Advertising

SwiftVPN does not use the content of your VPN traffic or browsing activity to target advertisements. Advertising services operate separately from the encrypted VPN tunnel.

5. Data Sharing

SwiftVPN may share limited technical information with service providers only when necessary to operate the application, including:

  • VPN infrastructure providers
  • Google AdMob and advertising partners
  • Hosting and technical service providers

SwiftVPN does not sell users' VPN traffic or browsing history.

6. Data Security

SwiftVPN uses encrypted VPN technology to protect traffic between your device and the VPN server. While reasonable measures are taken to protect the service, no internet-based service can guarantee absolute security.

7. Data Retention

SwiftVPN does not intentionally retain the content of users' VPN traffic or browsing history. Technical or diagnostic information, if generated, may be retained only for as long as reasonably necessary for security, troubleshooting, abuse prevention, or operation of the service.

8. User Choices

Users may disconnect from the VPN at any time. Where required, users may also manage or withdraw advertising consent through privacy and consent controls provided within the application.

9. Third-Party Services

SwiftVPN may use third-party services including Google AdMob. Their use of information is governed by their own privacy policies.

Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

10. Children

SwiftVPN is not intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the SwiftVPN service, advertising providers, legal requirements, or application functionality changes. The latest version will be available at this Privacy Policy URL.

12. Contact
